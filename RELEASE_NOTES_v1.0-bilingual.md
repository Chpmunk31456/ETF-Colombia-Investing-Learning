# ETF and Colombia Investing Learning Project — Bilingual Version 1.0

**Suggested GitHub release tag:** `v1.0-bilingual`

## Publication summary

This release presents a structured bilingual educational collection for learning about exchange-traded funds, Colombian investment options, cross-border planning, retirement cash flow, fraud prevention, and the property investment lifecycle.

The collection includes:

- 25 English learning lessons
- 25 Spanish learning lessons
- 31 Spanish practical CSV worksheets
- 19 Spanish reusable templates
- Bilingual navigation and a complete Spanish index
- Safety rules, decision controls, and educational disclaimers

## Covered learning areas

1. ETF fundamentals, costs, diversification, and index construction
2. Colombian savings, CDTs, fixed income, brokers, and platforms
3. US–Colombia cross-border tax and reporting considerations
4. Investment research, policy statements, and decision logs
5. Portfolio construction, retirement cash flow, and withdrawal sequencing
6. Fraud prevention, scam detection, and account security
7. Colombian property acquisition, financing, due diligence, rentals, renovations, and exits

## Intended use

These materials are intended for educational and family-learning purposes. They do not constitute individualized investment, tax, legal, lending, insurance, accounting, or real-estate advice.

Readers should verify all current market data, fees, tax rules, legal requirements, property records, platform status, and regulatory information through authoritative sources and qualified professionals before making financial decisions.

## Safety principles

- Keep emergency reserves separate from investments.
- Do not act under urgency or sales pressure.
- Reject guaranteed-return claims.
- Verify legal entities, regulators, custodians, and receiving accounts.
- Protect passwords, MFA codes, private keys, and recovery phrases.
- Use hypothetical information in worksheets before entering real personal data.
- Treat fees, taxes, currency conversion, and compliance costs as part of every decision.
- Use AI as a research assistant, not as the final authority.
