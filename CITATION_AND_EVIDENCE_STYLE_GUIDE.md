# Citation and Evidence Style Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines a practical citation method for the educational repository.

## Public Source Citation

Use:

`[Source ID] Organization, Document Title, date or effective date, section or page, accessed YYYY-MM-DD.`

Example:

`[SRC-001] Example Institution, ETF Factsheet, 2026-07-01, page 2, accessed 2026-08-01.`

## Private Source Citation

Use a non-sensitive reference:

`[PRIVATE-001] Account statement, reporting period ending YYYY-MM-DD, stored securely outside Git.`

Do not include:

- Full account number
- private portal URL
- personal address
- identification number
- password
- recovery code

## Claim Classification

Label important information as:

- Verified fact
- Estimate
- Assumption
- Opinion
- Interpretation
- Professional advice
- Unresolved

## Source Preference

Prefer:

1. Official primary source
2. Official institution document
3. Qualified professional opinion
4. Formal research
5. Reputable secondary source

## Dates

Record:

- Publication date
- effective date
- access date
- review date

## Sections

Identify the exact:

- Page
- section
- article
- clause
- table
- field
- statement line

## Estimates

For every material estimate, record:

- Method
- input sources
- range
- confidence
- review date

## AI-Assisted Content

AI output may assist drafting but must not be cited as the sole authority for:

- Current law
- tax obligations
- product terms
- property ownership
- insurance coverage
- professional licensing
- account facts

## Conflicts

When sources conflict:

- Cite both
- record the conflict
- identify authority and dates
- avoid unsupported resolution
- obtain professional review where material

## Publication Rule

A publication candidate should not contain unsupported material claims.
