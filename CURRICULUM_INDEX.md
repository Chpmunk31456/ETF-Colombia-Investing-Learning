# ETF Colombia Investing Learning — Curriculum Index

## Purpose

This index provides a consolidated navigation point for the educational curriculum.

The repository contains educational material only. It does not provide individualized investment, legal, tax, insurance, or property advice.

## Learning Paths

### ETF and Portfolio Foundations

Review the lessons in:

- `01-etf-fundamentals`
- `05-comparisons`

Topics include ETF structure, diversification, costs, screening, allocation, execution, rebalancing, and performance reporting.

### Colombia Investments and Property

Review the lessons in:

- `02-colombia-investments`
- `07-foreclosure-and-distressed-property`

Topics include Colombian investments, CDTs, brokers, property financing, closing, rentals, renovation, sale, and distressed-property controls.

### Cross-Border Planning

Review the lessons in:

- `03-cross-border-tax`
- Related planning lessons in `04-research-notes`

Topics include tax questions, reporting, currency, professional preparation, estate continuity, and cross-border coordination.

### Financial Planning and Governance

Review the lessons in:

- `04-research-notes`

Topics include investment policy, research, cash flow, balance sheets, liquidity, debt, goals, performance, governance, and simplification.

### Risk, Fraud, Privacy, and Insurance

Review the lessons in:

- `06-risk-and-scam-checks`

Topics include fraud detection, privacy, insurance, incident response, and professional due diligence.
