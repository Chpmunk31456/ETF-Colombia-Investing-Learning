# Questions for a Cross-Border Tax Professional

## Colombia

1. Am I currently a Colombian tax resident?
2. How are foreign dividends, interest, and capital gains taxed?
3. When is the foreign-assets declaration required?
4. How are assets held through the Mercado Global Colombiano classified?
5. Can foreign taxes be credited against Colombian tax?
6. How are exchange-rate gains treated?
7. How are Colombian CDTs and investment funds taxed?

## United States

1. How will Colombia-based investments be reported on my US return?
2. Could a Colombian ETF, collective fund, or pension product be treated as a PFIC?
3. Do my accounts require FBAR reporting?
4. Do my accounts require Form 8938 reporting?
5. How will foreign tax credits apply?
6. Are there estate, beneficiary, or inheritance issues?
7. Which records and exchange rates should I retain?

## Before Purchasing Any Non-US Fund

Obtain written tax guidance on possible PFIC classification and reporting obligations.
