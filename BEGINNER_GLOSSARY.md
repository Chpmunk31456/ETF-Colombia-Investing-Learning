# Beginner Financial Glossary Quick Reference

## Portfolio

**Asset allocation**  
How money is divided among categories such as cash, fixed income, equities, and property.

**Allocation drift**  
The difference between the current portfolio percentages and the intended target percentages.

**Diversification**  
Spreading exposure across different assets or risks.

**Rebalancing**  
Adjusting investments or cash flows to restore the intended allocation.

**Drawdown**  
A decline from an earlier portfolio high point.

## Money and Planning

**Net worth**  
Assets minus liabilities.

**Liquid net worth**  
Accessible financial assets minus relevant liabilities.

**Investable capital**  
Money available for investing after required reserves and obligations are protected.

**Emergency reserve**  
Money reserved for unexpected essential expenses or temporary income interruption.

**Sinking fund**  
Money accumulated for a known future expense.

## Investment Products

**Exchange-traded fund (ETF)**  
A pooled investment vehicle whose shares trade on an exchange.

**Certificate of term deposit (CDT)**  
A term-deposit product commonly used in Colombia under stated institutional conditions.

**Fixed income**  
Investments with contractual or structured payment obligations. The market value may still change.

## Trading

**Bid**  
The highest displayed buying price.

**Ask**  
The lowest displayed selling price.

**Bid-ask spread**  
The difference between the ask and bid.

**Market order**  
An order seeking execution at available market prices.

**Limit order**  
An order setting the highest purchase price or lowest sale price acceptable.

**Settlement**  
The formal completion of the exchange of cash and investments after a trade.

## Performance

**Total return**  
Price change plus investment income.

**Benchmark**  
A reference used to compare performance.

**Time-weighted return**  
A return measure designed to reduce the effect of contributions and withdrawals.

**Money-weighted return**  
A return measure reflecting the timing and size of the investor's cash flows.

**Nominal return**  
Return before adjusting for inflation.

**Real return**  
Return after adjusting for inflation.

## Property

**Market value**  
A supported estimate of what an asset may command in the market on a specified date.

**Property equity**  
Property value minus relevant secured debt and any included selling costs.

**Net operating income (NOI)**  
Property operating income minus defined operating expenses, generally before financing.

**Debt-service coverage ratio (DSCR)**  
Net operating income divided by debt payments.

**Vacancy**  
A period in which a rental property does not produce rent from an occupying tenant.

## Insurance

**Premium**  
The amount paid for insurance.

**Deductible**  
The amount the insured must bear under the policy before or alongside eligible insurer payment.

**Coverage limit**  
The maximum amount payable under stated conditions.

**Exclusion**  
A circumstance, event, or loss not covered by the policy.

## Governance and Safety

**Due diligence**  
Structured investigation and verification before a decision.

**Decision gate**  
A formal checkpoint that must be completed before proceeding.

**Stop condition**  
A circumstance that pauses or prohibits further action.

**Policy exception**  
An approved and documented deviation from a formal rule.

**Control**  
An action designed to prevent, detect, or correct risk.

**Multi-factor authentication (MFA)**  
Account security requiring more than one type of authentication.

## Important Distinctions

- Net worth is not investable capital.
- Property value is not property equity.
- Liquidity is not the same as safety.
- Account growth is not automatically investment return.
- Gross return is not net return.
- A policy limit is not guaranteed claim recovery.
- A promotional property listing is not an official legal notice.
- A professional title is not proof of current competence or authority.
