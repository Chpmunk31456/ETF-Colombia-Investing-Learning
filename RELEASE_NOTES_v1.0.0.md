## Purpose

ETF Colombia Investing Learning is an educational curriculum designed to help learners understand exchange-traded funds, Colombian investment alternatives, cross-border considerations, household financial controls, portfolio governance, fraud prevention, real-estate analysis, and long-term investment administration.

## Version 1.0 Scope

Version 1.0 contains 65 planned lessons covering:

- ETF fundamentals
- ETF costs and trading
- diversification and drawdowns
- index methodology
- Colombian investment options
- CDTs and fixed income
- broker and platform safety
- cross-border tax preparation
- distressed-property analysis
- household financial policy
- evidence and research governance
- portfolio construction
- fraud controls
- investment execution
- retirement-income planning
- real-estate financing and operations
- investment mathematics
- performance measurement
- household liquidity and debt
- financial continuity
- currency and cross-border transfers
- ETF domicile and withholding
- ETF liquidity and market stress
- derivatives and collateral
- fund closures and transitions
- risk metrics and scenario analysis
- custody and investor protection
- data quality and monitoring
- integrated capstone assessment
- final release governance

## Educational Disclaimer

This curriculum is educational and does not provide individualized investment, tax, legal, estate, accounting, insurance, brokerage, or real-estate advice.

No investment return is guaranteed.

Learners should verify current authoritative information and obtain qualified professional guidance before making material decisions.

## Privacy Warning

Blank templates are included for educational use.

Completed templates may contain sensitive personal and financial information and should not be committed to a public repository.

## Known Limitations

- Financial and tax rules may change.
- Product data can become outdated.
- Blank registers do not contain live market information.
- Automated QA cannot prove substantive educational accuracy.
- External links require periodic review.
- Localized or translated editions require separate QA.
- Graphical editions require additional accessibility review.
- Learners remain responsible for verifying product and professional suitability.
