- [ ] Communication with suspected fraudster stopped

- [ ] Scheduled payment cancelled where possible
- [ ] Institution contacted through verified channel
- [ ] Transaction hold requested
- [ ] Recall requested
- [ ] Affected card locked
- [ ] Affected account restricted
- [ ] Brokerage open orders reviewed

- [ ] Primary email password changed

- [ ] Email forwarding rules reviewed
