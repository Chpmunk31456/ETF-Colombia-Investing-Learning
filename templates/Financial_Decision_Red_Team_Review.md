## Challenge Questions

### Objective

Is the objective clear?

Could another action achieve it with less risk?

### Evidence

Which claim has the weakest evidence?

Which source has a conflict?

What evidence is missing?

### Assumptions

Which assumption is most important?

Which assumption has the lowest confidence?

What happens if it is wrong?

### Financial Risk

What is the maximum plausible loss?

Could the loss threaten essential reserves?

Are taxes, fees, and currency included?

### Fraud and Provider Risk

Who benefits from the transaction?

Are credentials and registration verified?

Are payment instructions independently verified?

### Liquidity

How quickly can the decision be reversed?

What happens if cash is needed early?

### Debt

What happens if rates rise?

What happens if income falls?

### Property

Could title, repair, vacancy, insurance, or sale costs invalidate the analysis?

### Behavioral Risk

Is fear, urgency, optimism, authority, or sunk cost influencing the decision?

### Status Quo

What is the cost of taking no action?

Is action truly required now?
