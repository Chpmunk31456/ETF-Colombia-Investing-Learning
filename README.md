## Project purpose

This repository is a structured educational system for learning about:

- Exchange-traded funds and portfolio construction
- Colombian savings, CDTs, fixed income, brokers, and investment platforms
- US–Colombia cross-border tax and reporting questions
- Retirement cash flow and withdrawal planning
- Fraud prevention and account security
- Property purchases, financing, rentals, renovations, distressed property, and sales
- Evidence-based investment research and decision documentation

The material is educational only. It is not individualized legal, tax, investment, lending, insurance, or real-estate advice.

## Recommended family learning sequence

1. Read the lessons in order.
2. Discuss the reflection questions together.
3. Complete the Spanish or English worksheets using hypothetical information first.
4. Build a paper portfolio before using real money.
5. Verify current facts through official sources.
6. Obtain qualified professional review for tax, legal, lending, estate, or property decisions.

## Core safety rules

- Keep emergency reserves separate from investments.
- Do not act under sales pressure or urgency.
- Never accept guaranteed-return claims.
- Verify the legal entity, regulator, custodian, and receiving account.
- Do not share passwords, MFA codes, private keys, or recovery phrases.
- Do not store sensitive personal records in this public repository.
- Treat fees, taxes, currency conversion, and compliance costs as part of every decision.
- Use AI as a research assistant, not as the final authority.

## Final readiness rule

A checklist does not make an investment safe or suitable.

Every real transaction must still be evaluated using current official documents, market data, fees, tax rules, legal status, property records, and the household’s actual financial circumstances.

When material evidence is incomplete, the correct status is:

**Research only — no transaction.**
