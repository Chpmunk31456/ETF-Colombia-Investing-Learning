# ETF and Colombia Investing Learning Project

[English](#english) | [Español](#español)

This repository is a bilingual educational system for learning about ETFs, Colombian investment products, cross-border tax and reporting questions, retirement cash flow, fraud prevention, and the full Colombian real-estate investment lifecycle.

> Educational material only. It is not individualized legal, tax, investment, lending, or real-estate advice.

### Core safety rules

1. Protect household liquidity and emergency reserves.
2. Verify legal entities, regulators, custodians, titles, and payment instructions independently.
3. Include fees, taxes, inflation, currency conversion, and worst-case scenarios.
4. Use primary and official sources.
5. Obtain qualified professional review when tax, legal, lending, estate, or property questions are material.
6. Treat “no transaction” as a valid decision when evidence is incomplete.

## Repository safety / Seguridad del repositorio

Do not commit or publish passwords, MFA codes, recovery phrases, private keys, complete account numbers, complete identification numbers, medical records, tax returns, bank statements, unredacted contracts, or tenant identity documents.

No publique contraseñas, códigos MFA, frases de recuperación, claves privadas, números completos de cuentas o documentos de identidad, historiales médicos, declaraciones tributarias, extractos bancarios, contratos sin anonimizar ni documentos de identidad de arrendatarios.
