# Investment and Broker Safety Checklist

- [ ] Legal entity name identified
- [ ] Regulatory registration verified
- [ ] Official website confirmed
- [ ] Custodian identified
- [ ] Investor-protection arrangements reviewed
- [ ] Deposit and withdrawal process understood
- [ ] Trading commissions documented
- [ ] Currency-conversion costs documented
- [ ] Custody or inactivity fees documented
- [ ] Tax documents provided by the broker understood
- [ ] No guaranteed-return claims
- [ ] No pressure to deposit immediately
- [ ] No request to send cryptocurrency to an individual wallet
- [ ] No remote-access software requested
- [ ] No unknown person has account credentials
- [ ] Two-factor authentication enabled
- [ ] Beneficiary arrangements reviewed
