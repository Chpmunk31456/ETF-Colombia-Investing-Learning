# Lesson 7 — Brokers, Platforms, and Account Safety in Colombia

## Learning Objective

Understand how to evaluate a broker or investment platform before transferring money, opening an account, or purchasing Colombian or international securities.

## Why Broker Selection Matters

A good investment can still produce a bad result if the broker or platform has:

- Excessive fees
- Poor execution
- Weak custody arrangements
- Limited withdrawal access
- Inadequate customer support
- Regulatory problems
- Weak cybersecurity
- Currency-conversion costs
- Restrictive transfer policies

The broker is part of the investment decision.

## Main Types of Access

A person living in Colombia may invest through:

- A Colombian bank
- A Colombian brokerage firm
- A local investment platform
- A collective investment fund manager
- A pension administrator
- A foreign brokerage account
- A platform offering access to international securities
- The Mercado Global Colombiano

Each route can differ in:

- Legal jurisdiction
- Product availability
- Investor protection
- Currency
- Fees
- Tax reporting
- Funding methods
- Withdrawal procedures

## Regulated Entity Versus Technology Platform

A polished app does not necessarily mean the company is a regulated broker.

The investor should identify:

1. The legal entity
2. The regulator
3. The custodian
4. The clearing or settlement entity
5. The bank receiving client funds
6. The jurisdiction governing the account
7. The legal owner of the securities

## Questions to Ask Before Opening an Account

### Legal and regulatory

- What is the full legal name of the company?
- In which country is it incorporated?
- Which regulator supervises it?
- Is the investment service provided by the same company shown in the app?
- Are there affiliated entities in other countries?
- Where are disputes resolved?

### Custody

- Who holds the securities?
- Are client assets segregated from company assets?
- What happens if the broker fails?
- Are securities registered in the investor's name, omnibus custody, or another structure?
- Can securities be transferred to another broker?

### Cash protection

- Where is uninvested cash held?
- Is it held in a bank account, money-market fund, or broker balance?
- Is deposit insurance applicable?
- Is there a maximum protected amount?
- Does protection apply to securities, cash, or both?

### Trading

- Which exchanges are available?
- Are trades routed to the original exchange?
- Are fractional shares used?
- Are orders executed internally?
- Does payment for order flow apply?
- Are limit orders available?
- Are extended-hours trades allowed?

### Fees

- Account opening fee
- Monthly fee
- Custody fee
- Inactivity fee
- Trading commission
- Bid-ask spread
- Currency-conversion spread
- Deposit fee
- Withdrawal fee
- International-wire fee
- Transfer-out fee
- Tax-document fee

## Commission-Free Does Not Mean Free

A broker may advertise zero commissions but earn money through:

- Currency spreads
- Bid-ask spreads
- Payment for order flow
- Interest on client cash
- Securities lending
- Premium subscriptions
- Withdrawal fees
- Internal execution

Always review the complete fee schedule.

## Funding From Colombia

Possible funding methods include:

- Colombian bank transfer
- PSE
- International wire
- Debit card
- ACH through a US bank
- Currency-conversion service

Questions to ask:

- Which currencies are accepted?
- What exchange rate is used?
- What is the conversion spread?
- How long do transfers take?
- Are third-party transfers allowed?
- Are deposits refundable only to the original account?
- Are withdrawal limits imposed?

## Withdrawal Testing

Before investing a large amount, consider testing:

1. A small deposit
2. A small purchase
3. A small sale
4. A small withdrawal

This helps confirm:

- Account ownership
- Processing times
- Fees
- Currency conversion
- Customer support
- Withdrawal reliability

## Colombian Versus Foreign Brokerage

### Colombian brokerage

Potential advantages:

- Local support
- COP funding
- Familiar regulatory environment
- Easier local documentation
- Access to Colombian products
- Potential access to selected international securities

Potential disadvantages:

- Smaller product selection
- Higher fees in some cases
- Lower liquidity in some securities
- Wider spreads
- Limited fractional-share availability
- Fewer ETF choices

### Foreign brokerage

Potential advantages:

- Broader ETF selection
- Direct access to major exchanges
- Lower trading costs in some cases
- Greater liquidity
- More research tools

Potential disadvantages:

- International transfers
- USD funding requirements
- More complex Colombian reporting
- Foreign-account reporting
- Cross-border estate considerations
- Customer support outside Colombia
- Possible account restrictions by residence

## Mercado Global Colombiano

When evaluating access through the Mercado Global Colombiano, confirm:

- Exact security available
- Original listing exchange
- Local trading currency
- Local trading volume
- Bid-ask spread
- Custody structure
- Tax classification
- Dividend treatment
- Currency-conversion process
- Whether transfers to another broker are possible

A foreign security accessed locally may still create foreign-asset reporting questions.

## Fractional Shares

Fractional shares allow the purchase of less than one whole share.

Potential benefits:

- Lower minimum investment
- Easier portfolio allocation
- Access to high-priced securities

Potential limitations:

- No transferability
- Broker-dependent pricing
- Limited voting rights
- Different execution methods
- Automatic liquidation when transferring accounts

## Securities Lending

Some brokers may lend client securities to other market participants.

Questions include:

- Is securities lending automatic?
- Can the investor opt out?
- Who receives the lending income?
- What collateral is used?
- What happens to voting rights?
- How are dividends treated?

## Cybersecurity Controls

Minimum account protections should include:

- Strong unique password
- Multi-factor authentication
- Login alerts
- Withdrawal notifications
- Device-management controls
- Beneficiary protection
- Session timeout
- Recovery-code storage
- Verified customer-support channels

Avoid SMS-only authentication when stronger methods are available.

## Scam Warning Signs

Do not proceed when a platform or representative:

- Guarantees returns
- Promises low-risk high profits
- Pressures immediate deposits
- Requests cryptocurrency transfers
- Uses personal bank accounts
- Refuses to identify the legal entity
- Avoids regulatory questions
- Requests remote computer access
- Asks for login credentials
- Claims losses are impossible
- Uses celebrity endorsements without verification
- Offers unusually high referral payments

## AI Trading and Bot Risks

An AI or automated trading platform may introduce:

- Model errors
- Overfitting
- Hidden leverage
- Excessive trading
- Unauthorized transactions
- Data-quality problems
- Cybersecurity risks
- Withdrawal restrictions
- False performance claims

Never give an unknown bot:

- Brokerage credentials
- API keys with withdrawal permission
- Remote access to a computer
- Authority to transfer funds
- Unrestricted trading permissions

## API Key Safety

When using a broker API:

- Create separate keys
- Disable withdrawals
- Restrict IP addresses if supported
- Use read-only access for research
- Rotate credentials
- Store keys outside source code
- Review activity logs

## Beginner Rules

1. Verify the legal entity before transferring money.
2. Test withdrawals before investing a large amount.
3. Compare total costs, not advertised commissions.
4. Keep emergency savings outside brokerage accounts.
5. Do not share passwords or unrestricted API keys.
6. Avoid guaranteed-return platforms.
7. Use multi-factor authentication.
8. Keep official statements and trade confirmations.
9. Review tax reporting before selecting the account.
10. Start small until the platform is proven reliable.
